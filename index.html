Missing rateMissing claims paid, as % of tracked cashback. At 10%, every ₹100 of tracked cashback brings ₹10 of missing claims with no commission behind them.
BufferThe part of Paytm's share set aside for missing claims. If claims cost more than the buffer, the gap comes out of Paytm's profit first.
Out of pocketWhen missing claims use up the buffer and all of Paytm's profit, Paytm pays the rest from its own pocket.
